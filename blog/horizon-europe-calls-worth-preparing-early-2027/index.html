Five Horizon Europe Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 23 September 2026.

The calls below span research and innovation actions, coordination and support actions, and a Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral co-funding scheme, covering topics from laboratory automation in food science to open access policy reform. Some are forthcoming and some are open, so planning timelines will differ across entries.

Automated Scientific Discovery: Food (RAISE pilot)

Open, closes 2 February 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.0 million per grant, one grant expected (2027).

This call specifically targets the food domain as a pilot for closed-loop scientific experimentation, meaning proposals must demonstrate how automated decision-making integrates with existing laboratory environments rather than building from scratch. The emphasis on trustworthy and safe systems signals that governance and reproducibility are as central as technical performance. Functional prototype demonstrators are an explicit deliverable, making this unusually concrete for a research and innovation action. The call also asks for development of best practices, which opens space for methodological contributions alongside experimental ones.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's existing laboratory infrastructure against the call's requirement for integration with current environments, and document specifically how your automated experimentation loop will produce reproducible outputs in a food-science context, since demonstrating that fit early will shape your entire technical description.

Official topic page: HORIZON-RAISE-2027-01-02

Next generation scenarios for informing climate and sustainability transitions

Opens 17 November 2026, closes 4 March 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 4.0 million per grant, about 6 grants expected (2027).

This call is explicitly oriented toward producing scenarios that serve diverse end-users at multiple spatial scales, from national policymakers to civil society, which means proposals must think carefully about how outputs will be communicated and used rather than only how they are generated. The description calls for stronger integration across research communities and better capture of interactions between climate and non-climate objectives such as biodiversity and pollution. Alignment with major global assessments including IPCC and IPBES is named as a concrete ambition, giving proposals a clear external anchor for impact. The emphasis on inclusive and globally representative scenarios also implies that consortium geography and stakeholder engagement will be scrutinised.

Preparation step. Identify at least two distinct end-user groups at different spatial scales (for example a planning authority and a civil society organisation) and draft a preliminary engagement plan that shows how your scenario development process will incorporate their decision-making needs, since the description foregrounds actionability and policy relevance as core outcomes.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2027-01-D1-13

Pillar I: Facilitating development of institutional open access policies through the retention of intellectual property rights

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 11 March 2027. HORIZON-CSA HORIZON Coordination and Support Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 2.0 million per grant, one grant expected (2027).

The call is directly tied to the ERA structural policy on open science and specifically addresses rights retention as a mechanism for enabling open access publications, a policy area where many research performing organisations still lack formal frameworks. It explicitly targets low-performing countries with respect to open access, suggesting that consortium design should reflect geographic balance across the EU and Associated Countries. Institutional repositories are named as a key infrastructure element, so proposals need to engage with how scientific information is preserved and made accessible at the organisational level. The framing as a Coordination and Support Action means the expected contribution is policy development and knowledge transfer rather than primary research.

Preparation step. Survey the open access policies of your intended partner institutions before drafting, paying particular attention to whether any currently lack rights retention clauses, because the call's stated outcome of increasing the number of organisations with well-developed policies implies your proposal should demonstrate a credible baseline and a measurable improvement pathway.

Official topic page: HORIZON-WIDERA-2027-05-ERA-01

Horizon scanning and foresight in future enabling digital technologies (CSA)

Opens 17 November 2026, closes 18 March 2027. HORIZON-CSA HORIZON Coordination and Support Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 4.0 million per grant, one grant expected (2027).

This Coordination and Support Action calls for the establishment of a forum that brings together a broad range of participants spanning science, engineering, research and technology organisations, and industry sectors, making the governance and facilitation model central to the proposal rather than peripheral. The description asks for alignment with national and regional initiatives, which means proposals should be able to show how the forum connects to existing innovation ecosystems rather than operating in isolation. The call also emphasises iterative and multidisciplinary approaches for co-creating future enabling technologies, signalling that process design matters as much as thematic content. Accelerating the uptake of novel advanced technology by industry and society is an explicit expected outcome.

Preparation step. Draft a forum governance model that specifies how participants from different disciplines and sectors will contribute iteratively over the project lifetime, and identify two or three national or regional initiatives your forum could formally align with, since the description treats that alignment as a structural requirement rather than a nice-to-have.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DIGITAL-EMERGING-10

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-Cofund-D HORIZON TMA MSCA Cofund Doctoral programme.

MSCA COFUND for doctoral programmes requires participating organisations to offer doctoral candidates international, inter-sectoral, and interdisciplinary experience, which means the programme design must build those dimensions in structurally rather than as optional add-ons. The call names enhanced employability both within and beyond academia as a key outcome, so training plans should address transferable skills explicitly alongside research competences. Increased global attractiveness and visibility of the participating organisations is also an expected outcome, meaning the proposal needs to articulate what makes the programme distinctive enough to attract international candidates. The co-funding model implies that the applying organisation must already have or be building a doctoral programme of sufficient scale and quality to absorb and benefit from the scheme.

Preparation step. Audit your existing doctoral programme against the three dimensions named in the description (international, inter-sectoral, interdisciplinary) and identify which of the three is currently weakest, then design a concrete programme element that addresses that gap, because the call's expected outcomes treat all three as non-negotiable features of a funded programme.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

Deadlines, budgets, and call identifiers are drawn from the EU Funding and Tenders Portal and verified against it. EU funding calls close at 17:00 Brussels time on the stated deadline, so confirm the exact cutoff for each call on its official topic page before finalising your submission timeline.
