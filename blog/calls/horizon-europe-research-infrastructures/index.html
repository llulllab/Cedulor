Strengthening the potential of the EOSC for knowledge valorisation and industry-academia collaboration (EOSC Partnership)

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-CSA HORIZON Coordination and Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 2.5 to 4.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2027).

Funds actions to strengthen the European Open Science Cloud as a driver of knowledge valorisation and industry academia collaboration, improving legal, technical and incentive frameworks so that EOSC resources can better support open innovation, deep tech startups and engagement with the private sector.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-01-EOSC-02

Preparatory phase of new ESFRI research infrastructure projects

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-CSA HORIZON Coordination and Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.5 to 3.5 million per grant, about 9 grants expected (2027).

Funds the preparatory phase of new research infrastructure projects on the ESFRI Roadmap, addressing legal, financial and technical issues to provide a solid basis for investment decisions and long term commitment by Member States and Associated Countries.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-DEV-01-01

Consolidation of the research infrastructure landscape: development of complementarities, synergies and/or integration between a set of pan-European research infrastructures

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 2.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 4 grants expected (2027).

Funds activities to develop complementarities, integration or tighter operational links between two or more pan-European research infrastructures such as ESFRI Landmarks and ERICs, aiming to consolidate and strengthen the European research infrastructure landscape and increase its efficiency and sustainability.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-DEV-01-02

Consolidation of the research infrastructure landscape: individual support for evolution, long-term sustainability and emerging needs of pan-European research infrastructures

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 4.5 million per grant, about 8 grants expected (2027).

Supports the evolution, long-term sustainability and reorientation of individual pan-European research infrastructures (ESFRI Landmarks or ERICs), including new services for wider user communities, reduced environmental impacts and strengthened capacity to address EU policy priorities.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-DEV-01-03

Access to research infrastructures, their resources and services: large-scale pilots for more integrated scheme across (sub)domains

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 35.0 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2027).

Funds large-scale pilots for more integrated access schemes across research infrastructure domains, providing single-entry point portals, converging access procedures and FAIR data management to enable researchers and innovators to use the best available facilities regardless of location.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-SERV-01-01

Access to research infrastructure services to enable R&I addressing EU priorities and emerging challenges

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 6 grants expected (2027).

Supports access to research infrastructure services for challenge-driven research addressing EU priorities and emerging needs, fostering cross-disciplinary collaboration, training a new generation of researchers and providing integrated catalogues and single-entry point access portals.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-SERV-01-02

Connecting research infrastructures and a wider user community across the European Research Area through access to advanced research infrastructure services

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.0 million per grant, about 4 grants expected (2027).

Funds wider and simplified access to advanced research infrastructure services across the European Research Area, aiming to connect scattered national and regional facilities, spread excellence, enhance researcher training and strengthen Europe's research competitiveness.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-SERV-01-03

Testing and optimising models of co-creation of advanced research infrastructure technologies

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 10.0 to 15.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2027).

Supports testing and optimising co-creation models for advanced research infrastructure technologies, fostering cooperation between research infrastructures and public or private organisations to strengthen deep-tech innovation ecosystems and EU technological sovereignty.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-TECH-01-01

Pioneering Destination Earth for a Sustainable Future: Large-Scale Pilots and Demonstrators

Opens 9 March 2027, closes 15 June 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 7.0 to 12.0 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2027).

Funds large-scale pilots and demonstrators using the Destination Earth system to address complex cross-sectoral problems caused or worsened by climate change, incorporating innovative AI methods and engaging real end users including decision-makers in policy, preparedness and response efforts.

Official topic page: HORIZON-INFRA-2027-TECH-01-02
