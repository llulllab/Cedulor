EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 24 October 2026.

This is the edition of 24 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls gathered here span a wide arc of European research and innovation priorities, from bioenergy infrastructure and urban social policy to territorial governance, cybersecurity for smaller enterprises, and environmental contamination science. Each represents a distinct strategic direction within the broader European funding landscape, and early preparation is essential to building a competitive proposal.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the full demonstration of regional solid biofuel value chains, from the sourcing of upgraded biogenic residues and wastes through to high-efficiency combined heat and power generation. It is explicitly aligned with the Renewable Energy Directive and positions regional actors, including smaller enterprises, as direct beneficiaries of knowledge creation and economic value. The dual focus on flexible renewable power and heat for local industrial processes makes it relevant to a broad coalition of regional stakeholders. Reaching EU climate and energy targets while advancing European competitiveness in the sector gives this call both policy urgency and commercial relevance.

Preparation step. Map the regional biogenic residue and waste streams available to your consortium and document how each feedstock meets the sustainability provisions of the Renewable Energy Directive, since compliance with that directive is a stated requirement of the call.

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

With over 1.2 million people estimated to experience homelessness in Europe and nearly half in chronic situations lasting more than two years, this call addresses a documented structural failure in social systems. It calls for evidence on the social infrastructure and services needed at neighbourhood level, making it a genuinely place-based and multidimensional intervention. The scope explicitly moves beyond housing-led approaches to require systemic interventions, which broadens the range of relevant disciplines and partner organisations. Projects are expected to improve both the availability and accessibility of key services for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods.

Preparation step. Identify two or three specific neighbourhoods where partner organisations already have operational relationships with homeless groups, and begin assembling baseline data on existing social infrastructure gaps at that local scale, since the call requires evidence grounded in neighbourhood-level realities.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call is explicitly framed as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, covering the period focused on scaling and wider deployment rather than early research. It invites territories including countries, regions, and interregional partnerships to design and demonstrate governance and regulatory experimentation environments. The emphasis on multi-layered governance and on turning analytical insights into replicable best practices means that institutional and policy actors are as central as technical ones. Projects are expected to contribute to multiple Missions simultaneously, which creates scope for cross-sectoral consortia.

Preparation step. Engage your regional or national authority partners well before the deadline to confirm their willingness to design governance and regulatory experimentation frameworks, since the call places territorial actors at the centre of the demonstration activity and their formal involvement shapes the entire project logic.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04

Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SME DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 5 grants expected (2026).

This call under the Digital Europe Joint Undertaking focuses specifically on deploying market-ready, automated cybersecurity solutions to smaller enterprises, a segment that is consistently underserved in cybersecurity capacity. It encompasses not only tool deployment but also employee training in the use of automated solutions, making it a comprehensive capacity-building action. The call explicitly encourages the use of results from previously supported European research and innovation projects, creating a direct pathway for mature technologies to reach new users. The integration of trustworthy automated tools into cybersecurity processes is a stated objective, giving the call a clear quality and governance dimension.

Preparation step. Audit the maturity level of the automated cybersecurity solutions your consortium intends to deploy and prepare a concise description of how each tool addresses the specific threats faced by smaller enterprises, since the call centres on market-ready solutions rather than further research and development.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME

Understanding the nature and the extent of the effects of PFAS on biodiversity and ecosystem services

Open, closes 14 January 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 5.7 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances represent one of the most persistent and pervasive classes of environmental contaminants, and this call seeks to close fundamental mechanistic knowledge gaps about their effects on biodiversity and ecosystem services. The scope connects directly to the Water Resilience Strategy, where addressing PFAS is identified as a flagship action, giving the call strong policy anchoring. Results are expected to be usable by policymakers in biodiversity protection, environmental regulation, and socio-economic cost-benefit analysis, which means proposals must be designed with knowledge transfer to non-scientific audiences in mind. The call also asks researchers to identify remaining research needs, making the scoping of knowledge gaps itself a deliverable.

Preparation step. Structure your research design to produce outputs at two distinct levels: mechanistic evidence on PFAS effects on specific biodiversity components and ecosystem services, and a clearly articulated map of remaining knowledge gaps, since the call treats both as explicit expected outcomes rather than treating the latter as a secondary consideration.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NATURE-2027-01-01
