EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 30 September 2026.

This is the edition of 30 September 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls gathered here span a wide range of research and innovation priorities, from border security and clean energy to social infrastructure, doctoral training, and deep-tech company scale-up. Cedulor has prepared this briefing to help research offices and applicants assess fit, understand what each call is really asking for, and take the right first step well before the deadline.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets concrete improvements in border surveillance, covering detection and characterization of unmanned vehicles including swarms, as well as situational awareness during large-scale population movements. The call explicitly links enhanced situational awareness to the better safeguarding of fundamental rights, which means technical and legal-ethical dimensions must be addressed together. Redundant detection and reaction capacities that protect both staff and society are a stated priority, signalling that operational robustness is as important as novelty. Teams that can demonstrate compliance with legal and ethical guidelines from the outset will be better positioned.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's existing detection and characterization capabilities against the full spectrum described in the call, land borders, sea borders, and swarm scenarios, and identify the specific gaps your project will close, since the description emphasises improved rather than wholly new capacities.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks call, specifically for Joint Doctorates, targets the quality and sustainability of doctoral training through cross-sector and cross-disciplinary cooperation. The expected outcomes address both the doctoral candidates themselves, in terms of transferable skills and employability, and the participating organisations, in terms of boosted research and innovation capacity and internationalisation. The Joint Doctorate format implies a particularly high level of integration between participating institutions, making the governance and supervision arrangements central to the proposal. Organisations that can demonstrate genuine complementarity across sectors and disciplines will have a stronger case.

Preparation step. Draft a supervision and training matrix that shows how each participating organisation contributes distinct disciplinary or sectoral expertise, since the call explicitly requires enhanced cooperation and transfer of knowledge between sectors and disciplines rather than parallel tracks within a single field.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action calls for full demonstration of regional solid biofuel value chains, from locally sourced sustainable biogenic residues and wastes through to high-efficiency combined heat and power conversion, in line with the Renewable Energy Directive. The emphasis on regional value generation and opportunities for small and medium enterprises means the call is looking for geographically grounded, economically coherent chains rather than generic technology demonstrations. Flexibility in delivering renewable power and heat to local industries is also explicitly required, pointing to real operational integration with industrial end-users. European knowledge advancement and international competitiveness in the sector are stated ambitions, so the proposal must connect regional specificity to broader impact.

Preparation step. Identify and document a concrete regional feedstock base of upgraded biogenic residues and wastes that demonstrably meets Renewable Energy Directive sustainability provisions, because the call requires the demonstration to be grounded in locally to regionally sourced material rather than assumed supply chains.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action focuses on structural, neighbourhood-level interventions to address homelessness, combining social infrastructure with coordinated services rather than relying solely on housing-led approaches. The call is explicit that multidimensional and systemic interventions are needed, and that evidence of the infrastructure and services required must be made available to stakeholders who work on homelessness. The scale of the problem, referenced in the description with over one million people experiencing homelessness in Europe and a high proportion in chronic situations, frames the urgency of producing replicable, evidence-based models. Improved availability and accessibility for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods is a direct expected outcome, so the project must demonstrate impact at that local level.

Preparation step. Define the specific neighbourhoods and the mix of social infrastructure and services your project will address, grounding the selection in the structural and multidimensional causes described in the call, so that the evidence produced is directly transferable to stakeholders tackling homelessness in comparable contexts.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator supports individual companies, principally small and medium enterprises and start-ups, through a combination of grant funding, equity investment, and Business Acceleration Services designed to help them scale high-impact innovations. The call is specifically oriented toward deep tech, meaning innovations built on scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs, where the long timeframe and high risk before returns are generated make conventional financing difficult to secure. The patient capital rationale is central: the Accelerator is designed to shorten the time to attracting full investment by bridging a funding gap that the market will not cover alone. Companies that can articulate both the depth of their technological foundation and the scale of the market opportunity they are addressing will be best placed.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear account of the scientific discovery or technological breakthrough underpinning your innovation and explain specifically why the risk profile and timeframe make it unsuitable for conventional financing, since the call's rationale for support rests precisely on that patient capital argument.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01
