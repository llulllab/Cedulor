EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 12 October 2026.

This is the edition of 12 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation ambitions, from training the next generation of doctoral researchers to scaling deep-tech companies and advancing frontier science. Reviewing each call's specific scope and expected outcomes well before the deadline will help your team build a proposal that is genuinely responsive rather than generically framed.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are designed to raise the quality and international reach of doctoral training by bringing together organisations across sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly targets improvements in transferable skills, employability both inside and outside academia, and the conversion of research ideas into products and services. Participating organisations are expected to demonstrate enhanced cooperation, knowledge transfer, and boosted research and innovation capacity. The internationalisation dimension is built into the programme's core logic, making cross-border and cross-sector consortia central to a competitive application.

Preparation step. Map the specific transferable skills gaps and cross-sector knowledge transfer opportunities your proposed consortium can address, and document how each participating organisation contributes a distinct disciplinary or sectoral perspective that strengthens the training programme's relevance and sustainability.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the practical demonstration of regional bioenergy value chains using locally sourced sustainable solid biofuels from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes, directly linked to the Renewable Energy Directive. High-efficiency combined heat and power generation is at the centre of the scope, with explicit attention to flexible renewable power and heat supply for local industries. The call connects regional value generation, competitiveness for smaller enterprises, and European knowledge advancement in a single demonstrator framework. Reaching EU climate and energy targets is named as a direct expected contribution, giving the call strong policy relevance.

Preparation step. Identify and document the specific biogenic residue and waste streams available in your target region, confirm their alignment with Renewable Energy Directive sustainability provisions, and outline how the proposed combined heat and power plant configuration will deliver measurable flexibility for local industrial heat and power consumers.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call responds directly to evidence that over 1.2 million people experience homelessness in Europe, with nearly half in chronic situations lasting more than two years. It calls for systemic, neighbourhood-level interventions that go beyond housing-led approaches to address the structural and multidimensional causes of homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services. The expected outcomes require both generating new evidence about what infrastructure and services are needed and demonstrably improving their availability and accessibility for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods. The neighbourhood scale is deliberate and central, not incidental, to the call's theory of change.

Preparation step. Select target neighbourhoods where you can demonstrate both a significant presence of homeless populations and existing gaps in social infrastructure, then design a data collection and stakeholder engagement plan that will produce the structural evidence the call explicitly requires.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is structured specifically for companies, principally small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups, that are developing deep-tech innovations built on scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs. The call recognises that such innovations require patient capital over long timeframes and often cannot attract private financing because of the risks involved. A distinctive feature is the combination of grant and investment funding alongside Business Acceleration Services, which together are intended to shorten the path to full-scale investment. The scope is explicitly oriented toward innovations with the potential to create new markets.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear and evidence-based account of the scientific discovery or technological breakthrough underpinning your innovation, and articulate specifically why the risk profile and capital requirements make it unsuitable for conventional private financing at this stage.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is aimed at principal investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports up to sixty months of work, with a defined maximum funding level and a provision for additional funding to cover further eligible costs when these are necessary and duly justified. The call is grounded in scientific excellence as the primary criterion, with the principal investigator's track record of independence being a central element of evaluation. The flexibility to request additional funding for justified cost categories makes it possible to propose ambitious programmes that require resources beyond the standard ceiling.

Preparation step. Compile a focused account of your independent research contributions to date, clearly distinguishing your own intellectual leadership from work conducted within larger groups, as evidence of independence is a named requirement of this grant type.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG
