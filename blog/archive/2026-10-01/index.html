EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 1 October 2026.

This is the edition of 1 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of domains, from border security and doctoral training to bioenergy, social infrastructure, and deep-tech company scale-up. Cedulor has mapped each call to a focused preparation step so that research offices and investigators can direct their effort precisely. Reviewing these entries well before any deadline will help teams allocate writing and partnership resources effectively.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call directly targets improved detection and characterization of unmanned vehicles, including swarms, involved in cross-border trafficking, as well as broader situational awareness during large-scale population movements. It explicitly requires compliance with legal and ethical guidelines and emphasizes protecting fundamental rights alongside operational security. The dual focus on technological capability and rights-based framing means proposals must satisfy both engineering and governance reviewers. Redundancy and staff safety are also stated outcome criteria, adding a reliability dimension that must be addressed in the technical approach.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's detection and localization capabilities against each stated outcome criterion, particularly the neutralization and swarm-detection requirements, and prepare a dedicated section documenting how the proposed solution complies with the legal and ethical guidelines referenced in the description.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks at the Joint Doctorate level represent one of the most structured forms of intersectoral and interdisciplinary doctoral training funded under Horizon Europe. The description emphasizes transferable skills, knowledge conversion into products and services, and boosted research and innovation capacity across participating organisations. Internationalisation and cross-sector knowledge transfer are explicit outcome expectations, meaning the network's geographic and sectoral composition will be scrutinised. The joint doctorate format also implies formal degree-awarding arrangements that require early institutional alignment.

Preparation step. Initiate formal conversations with all prospective partner universities and non-academic organisations about joint degree-awarding agreements, since the Joint Doctorate format requires institutional sign-off that typically takes longer to secure than a standard consortium agreement.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the demonstration of full regional value chains for solid biofuel, from sustainably sourced biogenic residues and wastes through to high-efficiency combined heat and power generation, explicitly aligned with the Renewable Energy Directive. The emphasis on regional sourcing and local industrial heat supply means the geographic and supply-chain logic of the proposal is central to evaluation. Opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises and regional value generation are named as expected outcomes, so the involvement of SMEs is not incidental but a substantive requirement. European competitiveness and energy security framing also signals that the call sits within a broader strategic context reviewers will expect applicants to acknowledge.

Preparation step. Develop a detailed regional value chain map showing the sourcing locations of biogenic residues and wastes, the conversion facility, and the local industrial heat consumers, and verify that each feedstock stream can be documented as compliant with the Renewable Energy Directive provisions cited in the description.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call addresses homelessness as a structural and multidimensional problem requiring systemic interventions at neighbourhood level, going beyond housing-led approaches to focus on social infrastructure and services. The description cites a significant proportion of chronically homeless individuals, underscoring the urgency of evidence-based structural responses. Proposals must generate evidence that is explicitly made available to stakeholders, meaning a dissemination and knowledge-transfer component is built into the expected outcomes. The neighbourhood scale is a deliberate design choice in the call, and proposals that operate at a broader or narrower spatial level will likely miss the stated scope.

Preparation step. Identify and formally engage the local authorities, social service providers, and community organisations operating in the specific target neighbourhoods, as the call's emphasis on structural social infrastructure at neighbourhood level means that place-based partnerships are central to demonstrating the feasibility of the intervention.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator provides a combined grant and equity investment instrument specifically designed for SMEs and start-ups with deep-tech innovations that require patient capital over long timeframes before returns materialise. The description explicitly acknowledges that such innovations struggle to attract private financing because of high risk and extended time horizons, positioning the Accelerator as a bridge to eventual full investment. Business Acceleration Services are part of the support package, meaning successful applicants gain access to more than funding. The short proposal format of this specific call is the entry point to a multi-stage process, so the quality and clarity of the initial submission is decisive.

Preparation step. Prepare a concise, evidence-based narrative for the short proposal that articulates the scientific or technological breakthrough underpinning the innovation and quantifies the scale-up funding gap that patient capital is intended to close, since these two elements are the explicit selection criteria described in the call.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01
