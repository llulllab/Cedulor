EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 14 October 2026.

This is the edition of 14 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation ambitions, from doctoral training networks to deep-tech company scale-up and frontier investigator grants. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help you match your organisation's strengths to the right opportunity well before the deadline.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are designed to raise the quality and international reach of doctoral training by bringing together organisations across sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly targets improved employability of doctoral candidates both inside and outside academia, making it relevant to consortia that combine academic and non-academic partners. It also emphasises the conversion of research ideas into products and services, which broadens the potential consortium composition beyond purely academic institutions.

Preparation step. Map your prospective consortium against the call's dual focus on intersectoral knowledge transfer and internationalisation: identify at least one non-academic partner whose involvement would directly strengthen the transferable-skills and employability outcomes described in the expected outcomes, and document how each participating organisation contributes a distinct training or research capacity.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the demonstration of full regional value chains for solid biofuel supply and conversion in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants, explicitly linking local biogenic residues and wastes to the provisions of the Renewable Energy Directive. The scope creates concrete market opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises and positions European actors for international competitiveness in the bioenergy sector. Its emphasis on flexible renewable power and heat for local industrial processes means the call rewards proposals with strong regional grounding and measurable supply-chain integration.

Preparation step. Before drafting, establish the geographic boundaries of your proposed regional value chain and collect preliminary data on locally available biogenic residues and wastes that qualify under the Renewable Energy Directive, so that the demonstration character of the project is credible from the first page of the proposal.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call addresses homelessness as a structural, multidimensional challenge requiring systemic interventions at neighbourhood level, going beyond housing-led approaches to focus on social infrastructure and coordinated services. The description cites concrete European data on the scale and chronicity of homelessness, signalling that the evaluation will reward proposals grounded in rigorous evidence generation. Delivering improved availability and accessibility of social infrastructure for homeless groups in specific neighbourhoods is the stated outcome, so the call suits partnerships that combine urban governance, social services and community-level implementation.

Preparation step. Select target neighbourhoods where your partnership already has operational relationships with stakeholders involved in tackling homelessness, and define upfront the evidence-generation methodology you will use to document which combinations of social infrastructure and services produce measurable improvements in availability and accessibility for homeless groups.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is specifically designed for small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups whose deep-tech innovations require patient capital over long timeframes before returns can be generated, a financing gap that conventional investors are reluctant to fill. The call combines grant and investment funding with Business Acceleration Services, making it structurally different from standard project grants. Its focus on innovations building on scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs means that the strength of the underlying research base is central to the evaluation.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear narrative of the technological breakthrough or scientific discovery underpinning your innovation, and articulate explicitly why the scale-up phase requires patient capital over a long timeframe, since the call description identifies that financing gap as the core rationale for Accelerator support.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is directed at Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports up to sixty months of work, with the possibility of requesting additional funding for eligible cost categories when duly justified in the proposal. This makes it one of the most substantial individual investigator awards available for researchers at the consolidation stage of their career.

Preparation step. Assemble documented evidence of your research independence well before the deadline, including publications, supervised researchers and any prior funding held in your own name, since the call description places research independence as the defining eligibility criterion for the Principal Investigator.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG
