EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 22 October 2026.

This is the edition of 22 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a range of research and innovation objectives, from doctoral training and frontier science to bioenergy demonstration and social infrastructure. Each entry distills what makes that specific call worth your attention and offers one grounded preparation step drawn directly from its description.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are designed to raise the quality and sustainability of doctoral training by connecting participating organisations across sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly targets improved employability within and outside academia, making it relevant to consortia that want to bridge research institutions with non-academic partners. Enhanced internationalisation is a stated goal, so networks with genuinely cross-border and cross-sector composition are well positioned. The emphasis on converting ideas into products and services also signals that industry co-supervision arrangements will be looked upon favourably.

Preparation step. Map the transferable-skills and cross-sector training components your consortium can realistically deliver, since the description places explicit weight on competences that improve career prospects both inside and outside academia, and document how each participating organisation contributes a distinct disciplinary or sectoral perspective.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the full value chain from locally sourced biogenic residues and wastes through to high-efficiency combined heat and power generation, meaning proposals must address both supply-side sustainability and conversion technology in an integrated way. The explicit alignment with the Renewable Energy Directive means that feedstock eligibility and sustainability criteria must be addressed from the outset. Regional value generation and opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises are highlighted as expected outcomes, so the consortium composition should reflect that ambition. Flexible renewable power and heat delivery to local industries is a concrete performance target embedded in the description.

Preparation step. Identify and document the regional biogenic residue and waste streams that will serve as feedstock, verifying their compliance with the Renewable Energy Directive's sustainability provisions, because the call grounds its entire value-chain logic in that regulatory framework.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

The call responds to a documented and large-scale social challenge: over 1.2 million people experiencing homelessness in Europe, with nearly half in chronic situations lasting more than two years. It focuses specifically on the neighbourhood level, calling for evidence of the structural social infrastructure and services needed rather than housing-led approaches alone. This framing means proposals must engage with systemic, multidimensional causes and demonstrate how coordinated interventions at that local scale can be made available and accessible to homeless groups. Stakeholders involved in tackling homelessness are an explicit audience for the evidence generated.

Preparation step. Select target neighbourhoods where you can credibly demonstrate improved availability and accessibility of social infrastructure and services, and establish relationships with the local stakeholders who will both co-design the intervention and receive the resulting evidence, since the description makes stakeholder engagement central to the expected outcomes.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is directed at principal investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are in the process of consolidating their own team or programme. The call supports excellent frontier research across all fields, with a grant duration of sixty months and the possibility of requesting additional funding for eligible cost categories when duly justified in the proposal. The combination of substantial resources and a long performance period makes this one of the most significant individual investigator grants available within the European research funding landscape. Researchers who can document a clear trajectory of independent scientific leadership are the intended applicants.

Preparation step. Assemble a detailed record of your independent research outputs and team-building activities to date, since the description states that evidence of research independence is a prerequisite for a Consolidator Grant principal investigator.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call is explicitly positioned as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, oriented toward scaling up and wider deployment of solutions rather than initial piloting. Territories including countries, regions and interregional partnerships in Member States and Associated countries are the primary actors called upon to design and demonstrate governance and regulatory experimentation. The multi-layered governance outcome and the emphasis on public-public and public-private arrangements signal that proposals without strong territorial and institutional backing will struggle to meet the stated expectations. Analytical insights are expected to translate into best practices replicable across a wide array of European Union funding instruments, giving successful projects a legacy beyond the grant period.

Preparation step. Secure commitment from the territorial authorities and governance bodies that will anchor the experimentation spaces, because the description frames multi-layered governance and the involvement of countries, regions or interregional partnerships as foundational to the scaling logic this call is designed to support.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04
