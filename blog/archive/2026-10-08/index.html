EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 8 October 2026.

This is the edition of 8 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation ambitions, from training the next generation of doctoral researchers to scaling deep-tech companies and advancing frontier science. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help your team identify the strongest fit and allocate preparation time wisely well before any deadline.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are designed to raise the quality and international reach of doctoral training by bringing together organisations across sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly targets improved employability of doctoral candidates both within and outside academia, making it relevant to consortia that want to bridge academic research with industry or public-sector partners. It also emphasises the conversion of research ideas into products and services, which broadens the potential impact beyond purely scientific outputs.

Preparation step. Map the cross-sector and cross-disciplinary composition of your prospective consortium early, since the call description centres on enhanced cooperation and knowledge transfer between sectors; identifying gaps in that mix well before the deadline will shape both the training programme design and the supervision arrangements.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the demonstration of fully sustainable regional bioenergy value chains, specifically the use of locally sourced solid biofuel from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants. It connects directly to the Renewable Energy Directive and to European energy security and competitiveness goals, giving proposals a clear policy anchor. The emphasis on flexible renewable power and heat for local industries signals that real-world industrial partners and regional stakeholders are central to a credible proposal.

Preparation step. Identify and engage regional suppliers of biogenic residues and wastes as well as local industrial heat consumers at an early stage, because the call's focus on regionally sourced feedstocks and flexible heat delivery to industrial processes means that the geographic and logistical coherence of your value chain will be scrutinised closely.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

The call responds to documented evidence that over 1.2 million people experience homelessness in Europe, with a substantial share in chronic situations lasting more than two years. It goes beyond housing-led approaches by requiring systemic interventions focused on social infrastructure and services at the neighbourhood level, which means proposals must address structural and multidimensional causes rather than immediate shelter alone. Generating evidence that can be made available to stakeholders tackling homelessness is an explicit expected outcome, so a robust knowledge-transfer component is essential.

Preparation step. Define the specific neighbourhood contexts where your intervention will operate and establish connections with the local stakeholders already involved in tackling homelessness in those areas, since the call requires that evidence of needed social infrastructure and services be made available to precisely those actors.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is designed for SMEs and start-ups that need a combination of grant and investment funding to scale innovations grounded in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs. The call specifically addresses the 'patient capital' problem: innovations that struggle to attract private financing because the risks and timescales are too high for conventional investors. Business Acceleration Services are part of the support package, making this relevant not only for technical development but for commercial scale-up strategy.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear articulation of why your innovation falls into the 'deep tech, patient capital' category described in the call, documenting the scientific or technological breakthrough at its core and explaining concretely why the funding gap exists, since the Accelerator's rationale is built around that specific financing challenge.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant supports principal investigators who are at the stage of consolidating an independent research team or programme and who can already demonstrate research independence. The call specifies a maximum grant duration of 60 months and allows additional funding for justified cost categories beyond the standard ceiling, giving investigators flexibility to design ambitious programmes. Because the instrument is built around a single excellent principal investigator rather than a consortium, the scientific vision and the track record of that individual are the core of the proposal. The pro rata temporis reduction for shorter durations means the full period should be used unless there is a strong scientific reason to propose less.

Preparation step. Compile a coherent narrative of your research independence, including outputs, group leadership, and the intellectual trajectory that makes the proposed programme a natural next stage, since the call's own scope makes that evidence of independence the foundation of a competitive application.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG
