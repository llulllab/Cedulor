EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 15 October 2026.

This is the edition of 15 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation activities, from doctoral training and frontier science to neighbourhood-level social infrastructure and deep-tech company scale-up. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help research offices and investigators match their strongest ideas to the most appropriate instrument. Preparation timelines differ considerably across these instruments, so building a realistic internal schedule well before the deadline is essential.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are a primary European mechanism for training the next generation of researchers through structured, cross-sectoral programmes. This call explicitly targets improvements in doctoral candidates' transferable skills, employability both inside and outside academia, and the capacity to translate knowledge into products and services. Participating organisations stand to gain through enhanced cooperation, knowledge transfer between sectors and disciplines, and increased internationalisation. The emphasis on boosting research and innovation capacity across the network makes this particularly valuable for consortia that combine academic and non-academic partners.

Preparation step. Map your prospective consortium against the call's dual focus on training quality and cross-sector knowledge transfer, ensuring that each participating organisation can articulate a concrete role in supervision arrangements and in broadening the doctoral candidates' career prospects beyond academia.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call addresses a specific and technically demanding challenge: demonstrating that regionally sourced solid biofuels, derived from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes and compliant with the Renewable Energy Directive, can feed high-efficiency combined heat and power plants. The expected outcomes are tied directly to European climate and energy targets, regional value generation, and international competitiveness, giving successful projects a clear policy and market relevance. The call also highlights opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises within these regional value chains, broadening the potential consortium base. Flexible renewable power and heat delivery to local industries is an additional dimension that strengthens the industrial case for the work.

Preparation step. Identify a concrete regional value chain where locally sourced biogenic residues and wastes are already available or could realistically be upgraded, and document how the proposed combined heat and power demonstration would meet the sustainability provisions of the Renewable Energy Directive before drafting the technical approach.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call responds to a documented and large-scale social challenge: over 1.2 million people estimated to experience homelessness in Europe, with a significant share in chronic situations lasting more than two years. Rather than focusing solely on housing-led approaches, the call explicitly calls for systemic interventions that address the structural and multidimensional causes of homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services at the neighbourhood level. Generating usable evidence for stakeholders tackling homelessness is a central expected outcome, making this as much a knowledge-production challenge as an implementation one. The neighbourhood scale of the intervention is a deliberate design choice that shapes both the methodology and the partnerships required.

Preparation step. Select target neighbourhoods where partners already have established relationships with local authorities and service providers, so the project can credibly demonstrate improved availability and accessibility of social infrastructure for homeless groups rather than building those relationships from scratch during implementation.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is specifically designed for small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups that need a combination of grant funding and equity investment to scale innovations that carry high risk over long timeframes, conditions that typically make them unattractive to conventional investors. The call's focus on deep tech, meaning innovations grounded in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs, sets a high bar for the novelty and ambition of the proposed work. Business Acceleration Services are offered alongside financial support, reflecting the programme's recognition that capital alone is insufficient for scale-up. Companies that can demonstrate patient-capital needs and a credible path to new or transformed markets are the intended applicants.

Preparation step. Before preparing the short proposal, build a clear narrative around why the innovation requires patient capital and cannot attract conventional financing at this stage, grounding that argument in the specific scientific or technological breakthrough that underpins the product or service.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant supports principal investigators who are at the stage of consolidating an independent research team or programme and who can already demonstrate research independence. The grant supports frontier research driven by the investigator's own scientific vision, with a duration of up to sixty months and the possibility of requesting additional funding for specific eligible cost categories when duly justified. The scale of the grant makes it one of the most significant individual investigator awards available through Horizon Europe, and the requirement to show evidence of independence means the track record section of the proposal carries substantial weight. Proposals of shorter duration receive a proportionally reduced maximum amount, so the planned scope of work should justify the full period.

Preparation step. Compile a structured account of your research independence, including leadership of your own research lines and outputs that are distinct from your doctoral or postdoctoral supervisors, since the call description identifies demonstrated independence as the defining criterion for a Consolidator Grant principal investigator and the evaluation will scrutinise that evidence closely.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG
