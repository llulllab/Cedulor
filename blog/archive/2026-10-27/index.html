EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 27 October 2026.

This is the edition of 27 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a broad range of thematic priorities, from bioenergy infrastructure and social innovation to frontier research and cybersecurity. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help research offices align their strongest competencies with the right opportunity well before the deadline.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets the full regional value chain for solid biofuels, from sourcing upgraded biogenic residues and wastes to converting them in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants. The call explicitly ties success to compliance with the Renewable Energy Directive, making regulatory alignment a central concern from the outset. It also frames the work as an opportunity for small and medium-sized enterprises and for generating regional economic value, which broadens the potential consortium profile considerably.

Preparation step. Map your region's available biogenic residue and waste streams against the Renewable Energy Directive's sustainability provisions, so that your proposal can demonstrate a credible and compliant local-to-regional supply chain before addressing the conversion technology itself.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

The call addresses homelessness as a structural, multidimensional problem requiring systemic interventions at the neighbourhood level, not only housing-led responses. It calls for evidence on the social infrastructure and services that actually reduce chronic homelessness, a condition affecting a substantial share of the homeless population according to the description. The neighbourhood scale is deliberate, making place-specific data collection and stakeholder engagement central to what the call expects projects to deliver.

Preparation step. Identify and engage the local or municipal stakeholders who can provide access to neighbourhood-level data on social infrastructure gaps, since the call requires that evidence of structural needs be made available to those involved in tackling homelessness.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for principal investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports ambitious, investigator-driven research across all fields, with substantial funding available for a five-year period and the possibility of requesting additional amounts for eligible cost categories that are necessary to carry out the proposed work. The requirement that any request for additional funding be duly justified in the proposal means that budget construction is itself a scientific and strategic task. For researchers at the right career stage, this is one of the most flexible instruments available for pursuing a self-defined research agenda.

Preparation step. Draft a clear narrative of your research independence to date, including leadership of projects, original lines of inquiry you have opened, and the trajectory of your current programme, because the description explicitly requires evidence of independence as a condition of eligibility for the Consolidator Grant.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action is positioned as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, explicitly focused on scaling up solutions rather than generating new knowledge from scratch. It calls on territories, including regions and interregional partnerships, to design and demonstrate governance and regulatory experimentation approaches that can be replicated across a wide array of European Union funding instruments. The multi-layered governance dimension means that proposals need to show how public-public and public-private arrangements can anchor mission-relevant solutions in specific territorial contexts.

Preparation step. Engage regional or interregional governance actors early to co-design the experimentation framework, because the call specifically requires territorial anchoring through multi-layered governance and expects analytical insights to be translated into replicable best practices.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04

Cybersecure tools, technologies and services relying on AI

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SIMPLE DIGITAL JU Simple Grants. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 4 grants expected (2026).

This Digital Europe Programme call focuses on deploying computational and automated cybersecurity tools that have been developed, tested, and validated under relevant conditions, with a clear expectation that outputs will be made available to Cyber Hubs, national cybersecurity authorities, and related bodies. The description emphasises enhanced information sharing and the production of original European cyber threat intelligence feeds, which points to a collaborative, multi-stakeholder delivery model. Compliance with European Union legislation is explicitly named as a requirement for the secure computational solutions to be developed.

Preparation step. Establish early contact with at least one Cyber Hub, national cybersecurity authority, or similar body named in the description, so that your proposal can demonstrate a credible pathway for deploying and validating the computational tools in operationally relevant conditions.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-CYBERAI
