EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 5 October 2026.

This is the edition of 5 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation priorities, from border security and clean energy to social infrastructure and doctoral training. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help your team identify the strongest strategic fit before committing resources to a full proposal.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets concrete improvements in detecting, localizing, and where appropriate neutralizing unmanned vehicles involved in cross-border trafficking, while also strengthening situational awareness during large-scale population movements. The explicit requirement to comply with legal and ethical guidelines, and to safeguard fundamental rights, signals that proposals must balance technical capability with governance considerations. Redundant detection and reaction systems that protect staff and society are a stated priority, making this relevant to consortia that combine technical and legal expertise.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's technical capabilities against the specific detection scenarios described, including both individual unmanned vehicles and swarm configurations, and identify which legal and ethical compliance frameworks your team can credibly address in the proposal narrative.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks support structured doctoral training that builds both research and transferable skills, with a clear emphasis on improving employability inside and outside academia. The Joint Doctorate strand requires participating organisations to integrate their training and research activities, making cross-institutional and cross-sector collaboration a design requirement rather than an optional feature. The expected outcomes for organisations include boosted research and innovation capacity and increased internationalisation, which makes this call attractive to universities and non-academic partners seeking to formalise long-term cooperation.

Preparation step. Draft a preliminary training programme outline that explicitly maps how each participating organisation will contribute distinct disciplinary or sectoral knowledge, since the call stresses enhanced cooperation, knowledge transfer between sectors, and integration of training activities across partners.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call focuses on demonstrating full regional value chains for solid biofuels derived from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes, with conversion in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants, all in line with the Renewable Energy Directive. The expected outcomes explicitly link regional sourcing and affordability to European competitiveness and energy security, which frames the proposal as both a technical demonstration and an economic development case. Opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises and regional value generation are highlighted, making this relevant to consortia that include industrial and regional stakeholders alongside research institutions.

Preparation step. Identify and document the specific biogenic residue and waste streams available within your target region, and confirm their eligibility under the Renewable Energy Directive's sustainability provisions, since the call grounds its expected outcomes directly in those provisions.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call addresses homelessness as a structural and multidimensional problem requiring systemic interventions at the neighbourhood level, going beyond housing-led approaches to examine the social infrastructure and services that enable lasting change. The description references data showing that a substantial share of homeless individuals experience chronic homelessness for more than two years, which frames the call as one demanding evidence generation rather than short-term pilot activities. Improved availability and accessibility of social infrastructure for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods is a concrete expected outcome, giving proposals a clear measurable direction.

Preparation step. Select target neighbourhoods where your consortium has existing relationships with local authorities or service providers, and begin gathering baseline data on current social infrastructure gaps, since the call requires making evidence available to stakeholders involved in tackling homelessness at that scale.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is designed specifically for small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups that need a combination of grant funding and equity investment to scale innovations that would otherwise struggle to attract private finance due to high risk and long time horizons. The call's focus on deep tech, meaning innovations grounded in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs, sets a high bar for the novelty and ambition of the underlying technology. Business Acceleration Services are included alongside funding, which means successful applicants gain structured support for scaling, not just capital.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear articulation of the scientific or technological breakthrough underpinning your innovation and the specific market-creation potential it represents, since the call's eligibility and evaluation logic centres on those two elements rather than on incremental product development.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01
