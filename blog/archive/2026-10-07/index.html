EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 7 October 2026.

This is the edition of 7 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide arc of European research and innovation priorities, from border security and doctoral training to bioenergy, neighbourhood investment, and deep-tech company scaling. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help research offices and applicants position their work with precision and allocate preparation time well before the deadline.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets a concrete operational gap: the detection, localization, characterization and, where applicable, neutralisation of unmanned vehicles and swarms used in cross-border trafficking, as well as broader situational awareness during large-scale population movements. The call explicitly links improved surveillance to the safeguarding of fundamental rights, making the ethical and legal framing a substantive requirement rather than a formality. Redundant, reliable detection capacities that protect both staff and society are a stated priority, pointing toward robust, field-tested technical solutions. Consortia that can demonstrate real-world border conditions in their test environments will be well placed.

Preparation step. Map your proposed detection and situational awareness technologies against each of the stated expected outcomes, including the legal and ethical compliance requirement, so that every element of your technical approach is traceable to a specific outcome listed in the call description.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks in the Joint Doctorate format require participating organisations to build genuinely integrated training and research programmes, not simply co-supervise candidates. The call emphasises transferable skills, cross-sector knowledge transfer, and internationalisation as outcomes for both the candidates and the organisations involved. The explicit attention to converting research ideas into products and services signals that academic-industry partnerships with a clear innovation pathway are particularly valued. Strengthening the supervision arrangements and the organisational integration across partners is therefore central to a competitive proposal.

Preparation step. Draft a supervision and training matrix that maps each participating organisation's specific contribution to the joint curriculum, demonstrating the integration of training and research activities that the call description identifies as a key expected outcome for organisations.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action calls specifically for the demonstration of regional value chains that use locally or regionally sourced solid biofuel from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes, processed in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants. Compliance with the Renewable Energy Directive is an explicit condition, not a background assumption, making regulatory alignment a substantive part of the technical design. The call also stresses flexible renewable power and heat supply to local industries, which means the demonstration must address real industrial process needs. Opportunities for small and medium enterprises and regional value generation are highlighted, suggesting that the consortium composition should reflect the regional economic ecosystem.

Preparation step. Identify a concrete regional supply chain, including specific sources of biogenic residues or wastes and an industrial or district heat off-taker, and verify that the feedstock pathway can be documented as compliant with the Renewable Energy Directive before building the technical case.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Understanding capital market dynamics for increased investment in New European Bauhaus projects in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Research and Innovation Action addresses a structural financing problem: small-scale New European Bauhaus projects for neighbourhood transformation face a mismatch with large, short-term, risk-averse investment funds. The call asks for research that produces a better understanding of both the demand and supply sides of capital markets as they relate to sustainable and inclusive neighbourhood projects. The scope is analytical and evidence-based, making it distinct from implementation-focused calls; the expected output is improved understanding that can translate into increased investment flows. Project promoters, public authorities and investors are all named as beneficiaries, so the research design should be legible to all three audiences.

Preparation step. Define a research methodology that captures both the demand side (project promoters and public authorities seeking capital) and the supply side (investment fund structures, return expectations, risk profiles) of the capital market dynamic described in the call, ensuring the analytical framework covers the size, diversity, returns and risk dimensions the description explicitly names.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-02

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is designed for companies, principally small and medium enterprises and start-ups, whose innovations rest on scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs and require patient capital over a long timeframe before generating returns. The combination of grant and investment funding alongside Business Acceleration Services is a distinctive feature described in the call, aimed at enabling innovators to attract the full investment needed for scale-up more quickly than they could otherwise. The call's focus on deep tech means that a credible scientific or technological foundation is a prerequisite, not an enhancement. Companies that have already validated their technology at some level but face a financing gap because of high risk and long return horizons are the intended applicants.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear articulation of the scientific discovery or technological breakthrough underpinning your innovation, and document the specific financing gap you face, showing why the risk level and timeframe involved have made conventional investment sources insufficient, since the call description frames both elements as the core rationale for Accelerator support.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01
