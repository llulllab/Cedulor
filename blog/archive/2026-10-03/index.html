EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 3 October 2026.

This is the edition of 3 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide arc of European research and innovation priorities, from border security and clean energy to social infrastructure, deep-tech scale-up, and frontier research. Cedulor has mapped each call to the preparation steps most directly grounded in its own description, so your team can focus effort where it counts well before each deadline.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

Border surveillance of land and sea frontiers is under growing pressure from unmanned vehicle threats, large-scale population movements, and the potential instrumentalisation of irregular migration. This call explicitly links improved situational awareness to the safeguarding of fundamental rights, making the legal and ethical framing as central as the technical one. Reliable, redundant detection capacities that protect staff and society are a core expected outcome, which means proposals must address both operational performance and compliance simultaneously.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's detection and localization capabilities against the full range of scenarios described: unmanned vehicles including swarms, large-scale border crossings, and conditions arising from climate or social stress, then identify the legal and ethical compliance framework your methodology will follow, since the description treats that compliance as a non-negotiable expected outcome rather than a secondary consideration.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the demonstration of complete regional value chains, from sustainably sourced solid biofuels through to high-efficiency combined heat and power generation, which means a single-technology proposal will not suffice. The description ties eligibility directly to the Renewable Energy Directive's provisions on biogenic residues and wastes, so regulatory alignment is built into the scope from the outset. Opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises and regional value generation are explicitly named, signalling that socioeconomic impact at the regional scale is expected alongside technical performance.

Preparation step. Draft a value-chain map that traces your solid biofuel feedstock from its regional source through upgrading and conversion to the final heat and power output, confirming at each stage that the feedstock classification and sustainability criteria align with the Renewable Energy Directive provisions the description references directly.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

With over 1.2 million people estimated to experience homelessness in Europe and nearly half of surveyed individuals in chronic situations lasting more than two years, the scale of the problem described here demands systemic rather than piecemeal responses. The call explicitly requires evidence of the structural social infrastructure and services needed at neighbourhood level, which positions rigorous data collection and stakeholder engagement as core deliverables rather than supporting activities. Improving both availability and accessibility of services for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods must be demonstrated concretely.

Preparation step. Identify the specific neighbourhoods where your project will intervene and begin assembling baseline data on existing social infrastructure gaps there, since the description requires that evidence of structural needs be made available to stakeholders and that improvements in availability and accessibility be demonstrable outcomes of the work.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is designed for companies, principally small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups, whose innovations are grounded in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs and require patient capital over a long timeframe before returns can be generated. The combination of grant and investment funding alongside Business Acceleration Services is a distinctive feature described in the work programme, and it is explicitly intended to help innovators attract the full investment needed for scale-up in a shorter period. The focus on deep tech innovations that struggle to attract conventional financing makes this route particularly relevant for ventures where commercial risk and development timelines deter standard investors.

Preparation step. Review Section VII of the EIC Work Programme 2026 on Business Acceleration Services, as the description names it directly, and prepare a clear articulation of why your innovation meets the patient-capital rationale: specify the scientific or technological breakthrough at its core, the funding gap that conventional investors have declined to fill, and the scale-up trajectory the combined grant and investment would enable.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is directed at principal investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports ambitious, investigator-driven research over a five-year period, with the possibility of requesting additional funding for eligible cost categories when the scientific case justifies it. The requirement that any request for additional funding be duly justified in the proposal means that budget construction is itself a scientific and strategic exercise, not merely an administrative one. Researchers who have established a distinctive research direction and can show a coherent programme of work are the intended applicants.

Preparation step. Assemble documented evidence of your research independence (publications, supervised researchers, externally funded projects) and use that record to shape the narrative of how the proposed programme consolidates and extends a trajectory that is distinctly your own, since the description places demonstrated independence at the core of eligibility.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG
