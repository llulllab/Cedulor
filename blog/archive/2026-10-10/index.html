EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 10 October 2026.

This is the edition of 10 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a range of programmes and action types, from researcher training and frontier science to innovation demonstration and company scale-up. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help your team identify the best fit and allocate preparation time well before the deadline.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are designed to raise the quality and international reach of doctoral training by bringing together organisations across sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly targets improvements in transferable skills, employability both inside and outside academia, and the conversion of research ideas into products and services. Participating organisations benefit from enhanced cooperation, knowledge transfer, and boosted research and innovation capacity. The emphasis on internationalisation makes this particularly relevant for consortia seeking to build structured, cross-border training programmes.

Preparation step. Map the disciplinary and sectoral mix of your intended consortium against the call's explicit goals of cross-sector knowledge transfer and increased integration of training and research activities, so that each partner's role directly advances those stated outcomes from the outset.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call addresses a concrete gap in the renewable energy landscape by focusing on the full value chain from locally sourced biogenic residues and wastes through to high-efficiency combined heat and power generation. It is framed around compliance with the Renewable Energy Directive and the creation of regional economic value, making it relevant to both technology developers and regional industrial actors. The expected outcomes explicitly include flexible renewable power and heat supply for local industrial processes, pointing to a strong demonstration and real-world deployment ambition. European competitiveness and energy security are named as direct motivations, giving proposals a clear policy anchor.

Preparation step. Identify a specific regional value chain where sustainable solid biofuel feedstocks from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes are already available or feasibly sourceable, and document how the proposed combined heat and power plant configuration would meet the high-efficiency threshold implied by the call's focus on efficiency demonstration.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

The call responds to a documented and large-scale social challenge: over 1.2 million people experiencing homelessness across Europe, with nearly half in chronic situations lasting more than two years. It moves beyond housing-led approaches alone, calling for systemic interventions that address the structural and multidimensional causes of homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services at the neighbourhood level. The expected outcomes centre on generating evidence that is genuinely useful to stakeholders tackling homelessness, and on improving the practical availability and accessibility of services for affected groups. This makes it well suited to consortia that combine social research capacity with direct operational presence in urban neighbourhoods.

Preparation step. Select target neighbourhoods where partners have existing relationships with local authorities and service providers, and begin assembling baseline data on current social infrastructure gaps for homeless populations, since the call's scope centres on producing evidence that is directly actionable for stakeholders at that local level.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is specifically constructed for small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups whose innovations are grounded in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs and require patient capital over a long timeframe before generating returns. The combination of grant and investment funding alongside Business Acceleration Services is designed to help companies attract the full investment needed for scale-up more quickly than would otherwise be possible. The call is explicit that such deep-tech innovations often struggle with conventional financing because of the risk profile and time horizons involved. For eligible companies, this makes the Accelerator a structurally distinct funding route rather than a conventional grant mechanism.

Preparation step. Before drafting the short proposal, prepare a clear articulation of the scientific or technological breakthrough underpinning your innovation and a realistic account of why the risk profile and capital requirements make conventional private financing insufficient at this stage, since the call's own scope makes those two elements central to the evaluation logic.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is aimed at Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports up to sixty months of work and allows for additional funding requests when these are necessary and duly justified for carrying out the proposed research. The scale of funding available reflects the ambition of supporting substantial, sustained programmes of frontier research led by a single investigator. This makes it particularly relevant for researchers who have moved beyond their initial postdoctoral phase and can demonstrate a coherent, self-directed research vision.

Preparation step. Compile a structured record of your outputs and activities that demonstrate research independence, since the call's scope makes evidence of independence a defining eligibility criterion, and ensure that any plan for additional funding beyond the standard maximum is accompanied by a justification tied directly to the scientific requirements of the proposed work.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG
