EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 26 October 2026.

This is the edition of 26 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation priorities, from geothermal resource assessment and neighbourhood investment dynamics to frontier research grants, mission-driven territorial experimentation, and cybersecurity capacity building for small and medium-sized enterprises. Cedulor has mapped each call to the preparation steps most directly grounded in its own description, so your team can focus effort where it counts most. Use this briefing to align your consortium or individual research strategy well before the deadline.

Resource assessment for deep sedimentary and basement reservoirs

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 4.5 million per grant, about 4 grants expected (2026).

Deep sedimentary and basement reservoirs remain largely unexplored because hydrocarbon industries have historically bypassed them, leaving a significant gap in the knowledge base for geothermal developers. This call directly targets that gap by funding research into reservoir structures and properties that can support approaches such as Enhanced Geothermal Systems and closed-loop geothermal systems. Success here would increase drilling success rates, improve geothermal source performance, and reduce the risk of induced seismicity, making deployment more environmentally responsible. For teams working on subsurface characterisation or geothermal engineering, this is a well-defined research frontier with clear societal relevance.

Preparation step. Assemble the geological and geophysical data your consortium already holds on deep sedimentary or basement formations, identify the specific permeability and structural prediction challenges you will address, and map those challenges explicitly onto the outcomes listed in the description, particularly increased drilling success rate and reduced induced seismicity risk.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-06

Understanding capital market dynamics for increased investment in New European Bauhaus projects in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

Investment in neighbourhood transformation aligned with the New European Bauhaus is being held back by a structural mismatch between the scale and risk profile of available capital and the characteristics of small-scale, sustainability-oriented projects. This call asks researchers to investigate the demand and supply dynamics of those capital markets so that project promoters, public authorities, and investors can make better-informed decisions. The scope is explicit that short-term, risk-averse investment funds are a central obstacle, making this a call for rigorous financial and economic analysis rather than purely technical research. Teams with expertise in urban finance, investment behaviour, or sustainable neighbourhood development are well positioned here.

Preparation step. Develop a clear analytical framework for characterising the capital market discrepancy described in the scope, distinguishing between demand-side factors (project size, diversity, risk profile) and supply-side factors (fund orientation, return expectations), and identify the empirical data sources your project will use to study investment dynamics in NEB-aligned neighbourhood projects.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-02

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports ambitious, investigator-driven research over a period of up to sixty months, with substantial funding that can be supplemented for specific eligible cost categories when duly justified. This makes it one of the most significant individual research grants available for mid-career researchers seeking to establish a distinctive scientific direction. The emphasis on evidence of independence means the scientific track record of the Principal Investigator is the central evaluative criterion.

Preparation step. Prepare a concise narrative of your research independence to date, drawing on your publication record, prior grants held in your own name, and any group leadership experience, then use that narrative as the foundation for articulating the scientific vision and ambition of the proposed programme in a way that demonstrates consolidation rather than continuation of someone else's agenda.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call is explicitly positioned as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, oriented toward scaling up and wider deployment of solutions that have already shown promise. Territories including countries, regions, and interregional partnerships are invited to design and demonstrate governance structures and regulatory experimentation spaces that can serve as replicable models across a broad array of European Union funding instruments. The multi-layered governance dimension and the emphasis on public-public and public-private collaboration make this a call for actors who can bridge analytical insight and territorial practice. Projects that succeed here are expected to generate best practices with genuine replication potential.

Preparation step. Identify the specific European Union Mission or Missions your territory intends to address, document the governance structures and experimentation spaces already in place or feasible to establish, and articulate how the insights generated will be transferable to other territories and funding instruments, since replicability is a stated outcome of the call.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04

Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SME DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 5 grants expected (2026).

This call under the Digital Europe programme targets the practical adoption of market-ready, computationally powered cybersecurity solutions, with a particular focus on small and medium-sized enterprises that are often underserved by existing security tools. The description emphasises deployment and market uptake rather than fundamental research, and it explicitly includes training employees to use computational solutions for cybersecurity as part of the scope. There is also a clear expectation that solutions may draw on results from prior Horizon Europe projects, creating a pathway for mature research outputs to reach end users. Organisations that can demonstrate trustworthy, computationally driven tools ready for SME environments are the primary target.

Preparation step. Audit the computational cybersecurity solutions your consortium or partners have already developed or validated, confirm their market readiness for SME deployment, and prepare a deployment and dissemination plan that addresses the three dimensions named in the description: preparing organisations, protecting them, and enabling response to cybersecurity threats.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME
