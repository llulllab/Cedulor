EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 2 October 2026.

This is the edition of 2 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation priorities, from border security and clean energy to social infrastructure and doctoral training. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully is the most productive way to assess fit before committing resources to a proposal.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets concrete improvements in border surveillance, including the detection and characterisation of unmanned vehicles operating alone or in swarms, as well as enhanced situational awareness during large-scale cross-border movements of people. The call explicitly links technological capability to the protection of fundamental rights, making it relevant to consortia that can address both the technical and ethical dimensions of border management. Redundant detection and reaction systems that keep staff and society safe are a stated priority, pointing toward robust, field-tested solutions rather than early-stage research.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's technical capabilities against the specific detection scenarios described, particularly counter-unmanned-vehicle systems and large-scale migration monitoring, and prepare documentation showing how your approach complies with the legal and ethical guidelines the call explicitly requires.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks in the Joint Doctorate format are designed to raise the quality and international reach of doctoral training by integrating research activities across participating organisations from different sectors and disciplines. The call emphasises transferable skills, employability both inside and outside academia, and the capacity to convert research ideas into products and services where relevant. Boosting the internationalisation of doctoral programmes and deepening inter-sectoral knowledge transfer are explicit goals, making this a strong vehicle for consortia that can offer genuinely cross-boundary supervision arrangements.

Preparation step. Identify partner organisations from at least two different sectors or disciplines whose research agendas complement one another, and begin drafting a joint supervision framework that demonstrates how doctoral candidates will gain both research depth and transferable competences across the network.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action calls for full demonstration of regional bioenergy value chains that use locally or regionally sourced sustainable solid biofuels, including upgraded biogenic residues and wastes, in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants. The call connects directly to the Renewable Energy Directive and frames success in terms of regional value generation, SME opportunities, and European competitiveness in the sector. Providing flexible renewable power and heat to local industries is a concrete expected outcome, meaning the project must go beyond laboratory or pilot scale to show real operational performance.

Preparation step. Secure commitments from regional waste and residue suppliers and an industrial heat consumer well before the deadline, so that the demonstration of a complete value chain, from feedstock sourcing through CHP conversion, can be substantiated in the proposal with credible operational data.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action responds to evidence that over 1.2 million people experience homelessness in Europe, with a large share facing chronic homelessness lasting more than two years. The call focuses on the neighbourhood level, asking projects to generate evidence about the structural social infrastructure and services needed and to improve their actual availability and accessibility for homeless groups in targeted areas. The scope makes clear that housing-led approaches alone are insufficient and that systemic, multidimensional interventions are required.

Preparation step. Define the specific neighbourhoods where the intervention will take place and engage local stakeholders, including social service providers and municipal authorities, early enough to build the evidence base about existing infrastructure gaps that the call explicitly asks projects to produce.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is designed for individual companies, principally SMEs and start-ups, that are developing high-impact innovations grounded in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs and that require patient capital over a long timeframe before returns materialise. The programme offers a distinctive combination of grant funding, equity investment, and Business Acceleration Services, addressing the financing gap that deep-tech innovators typically face when commercial investors consider the risk and timeline too high. The call is explicitly oriented toward scale-up rather than early research, so readiness to demonstrate market potential is central.

Preparation step. Before drafting the short proposal, prepare a clear articulation of the technological breakthrough underpinning your innovation and a realistic scale-up plan that explains why the combination of grant and investment funding, rather than conventional financing alone, is necessary given the risk profile and timeframe involved.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01
