EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 28 October 2026.

This is the edition of 28 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span energy demonstration, social innovation, frontier research, mission-driven territorial governance, and digital security for smaller enterprises. Reviewing each call description carefully before beginning your proposal will help you match your consortium's strengths to the specific outcomes each programme expects.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets the full chain from biogenic residue collection to high-efficiency combined heat and power generation, meaning it rewards proposals that integrate supply-side and conversion-side expertise together rather than addressing either in isolation. The explicit alignment with the Renewable Energy Directive signals that regulatory compliance and sustainability documentation will be scrutinised closely. Opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises are highlighted in the description, making this an attractive entry point for regional actors who can demonstrate locally sourced feedstocks and flexible heat and power delivery to industrial processes.

Preparation step. Map the regional availability of upgraded biogenic residues and wastes in your target area and document how their sourcing meets Renewable Energy Directive sustainability provisions, since the call explicitly requires this alignment as a foundation for the demonstration case.

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action addresses the structural and multidimensional causes of homelessness at neighbourhood level, going beyond housing-led approaches to focus on social infrastructure and coordinated services. The description references concrete European-scale data on chronic homelessness, which frames the expected evidence base that projects must generate and make available to stakeholders. The neighbourhood scale is deliberate and specific, meaning proposals need to demonstrate genuine embeddedness in defined local contexts rather than city-wide or national abstraction.

Preparation step. Identify and engage neighbourhood-level stakeholders, including local service providers and social infrastructure operators, well before the deadline, so that the project can credibly demonstrate the coordinated multi-actor approach the call description requires for generating transferable evidence.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The Consolidator Grant is designed for Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are consolidating their own team or programme, making the stage of career development a central eligibility consideration. The grant supports a period of up to sixty months, giving researchers the runway to pursue ambitious, longer-horizon scientific questions that shorter funding cycles cannot accommodate. Additional funding beyond the principal amount can be requested for eligible cost categories when justified by the proposed work, which allows for genuinely complex experimental or computational programmes. Because the grant is awarded to an individual Principal Investigator rather than a consortium, the quality and originality of the research vision carries the full evaluative weight.

Preparation step. Draft a concise research vision statement that articulates the scientific independence you have established since your doctorate and the specific frontier question your consolidation phase will address, because the call centres evaluation on demonstrated independence and the ambition of the proposed programme.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action is positioned as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, which the description defines as focused on scaling up and wider deployment rather than initial piloting. The call explicitly invites territories, including countries, regions and interregional partnerships, to design and demonstrate governance and regulatory experimentation frameworks, which means the governance architecture of a proposal is as important as any technical content. The emphasis on replication across a wide array of European Union funding instruments signals that the Commission expects outputs with practical transferability, not just site-specific results.

Preparation step. Design the governance model for your territorial partnership early, drawing on the call's explicit reference to multi-layered governance and public-public and public-private arrangements, so that the proposal can demonstrate a credible structure for regulatory experimentation and replication from the outset.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04

Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SME DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 5 grants expected (2026).

This Digital Europe Joint Undertaking action focuses on deploying market-ready, automated cybersecurity solutions to organisations, with particular emphasis on small and medium-sized enterprises that typically lack in-house security capacity. The description specifically calls for integration of automated technologies into cybersecurity processes and for innovative employee training approaches, which means proposals must address both the technical deployment and the human adoption dimensions. Results from Horizon Europe projects are explicitly mentioned as eligible sources for the solutions to be deployed, creating a direct pathway for teams who have already developed relevant tools under earlier funding.

Preparation step. Audit the maturity and market-readiness of the automated cybersecurity tools your consortium plans to deploy, since the call description centres on solutions that are already ready for uptake rather than those still in development, and document any prior Horizon Europe project origins if applicable.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME
