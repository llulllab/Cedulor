EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 6 October 2026.

This is the edition of 6 October 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a broad range of research and innovation priorities, from border security and doctoral training to bioenergy, social infrastructure, and deep-tech company scale-up. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help your team identify the best strategic fit and allocate preparation time well before the deadline.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets concrete improvements in the detection, localization, characterization, and where applicable neutralisation of unmanned vehicles, including swarms, used in cross-border trafficking and smuggling. It also addresses large-scale movements of people driven by conflict, economic stress, and climate pressures, making it directly relevant to teams working on sensor systems, situational awareness platforms, or border management tools. The explicit requirement to comply with legal and ethical guidelines, and to safeguard fundamental rights, signals that proposals must integrate a robust rights-compliance dimension alongside the technical work. Reliable, redundant detection capacities that protect staff and society are a stated priority, so solutions must demonstrate operational resilience.

Preparation step. Map your consortium's technical capabilities against the full spectrum of expected outcomes, paying particular attention to how your detection or awareness solution addresses both unmanned vehicle threats and large-scale population movements, and prepare a clear account of how fundamental rights compliance and ethical guidelines are embedded in the system design from the outset.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks at the Joint Doctorate level represent one of the most structured mechanisms for building cross-sectoral and cross-disciplinary doctoral training capacity in Europe. The expected outcomes emphasise transferable skills, improved employability inside and outside academia, and the conversion of research ideas into products and services, which means proposals must articulate a training programme that goes well beyond disciplinary depth. Participating organisations are expected to demonstrate enhanced cooperation, knowledge transfer between sectors, and increased internationalisation, so the network architecture itself is a core deliverable. Boosting research and innovation capacity through integrated training and research activities is explicitly named as a goal.

Preparation step. Begin assembling a multi-sector, multi-country network of participating organisations whose complementary expertise directly supports the joint doctoral programme you intend to design, and draft a preliminary training structure that maps each partner's contribution to the transferable-skills and knowledge-conversion outcomes described in the call.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call focuses on demonstrating full regional value chains for locally sourced solid biofuel from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes, converted in high-efficiency combined heat and power plants, which places it squarely at the intersection of renewable energy targets, energy security, and regional economic development. The emphasis on compliance with the Renewable Energy Directive means that the sustainability credentials of the feedstock must be rigorously documented throughout the chain. Opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises and for regional value generation are explicitly highlighted, so proposals that embed local economic actors will be better aligned with the call's intent. Providing flexible renewable power and heat to local industries, enabling high penetration of renewables, is a concrete functional target that the demonstration must address.

Preparation step. Identify and engage regional feedstock suppliers, waste processors, and industrial heat consumers early, so that the demonstration value chain you describe in the proposal is grounded in real supply agreements and documented sustainability credentials consistent with the Renewable Energy Directive requirements the call names.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call responds directly to the scale of homelessness across Europe, with the description citing over 1.2 million people affected and noting that 45 percent of surveyed homeless individuals have experienced chronic homelessness for more than two years. The focus is on structural and multidimensional causes, requiring systemic interventions at neighbourhood level that combine social infrastructure with coordinated services, rather than individual-level housing solutions alone. Generating evidence that is usable by the range of stakeholders involved in tackling homelessness is a primary expected outcome, so the proposal must include a credible knowledge-production and dissemination component. Improved availability and accessibility of key services for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods is the operational test against which results will be measured.

Preparation step. Define the specific neighbourhood contexts in which your intervention will operate and establish partnerships with local authorities, service providers, and community organisations whose involvement will allow you to both implement coordinated social infrastructure improvements and generate the stakeholder-ready evidence base the call explicitly requires.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

EIC Accelerator 2026 - Short proposal

Open, closes 17 December 2026. HORIZON-EIC-ACC HORIZON EIC Accelerator.

The European Innovation Council Accelerator is designed for small and medium-sized enterprises and start-ups that need a combination of grant and equity investment to scale innovations that are too risky or capital-intensive to attract conventional financing, particularly those rooted in scientific discovery or technological breakthroughs. The call explicitly acknowledges that deep-tech innovations often require patient capital over long timeframes before returns materialise, and the Accelerator's blended finance model is constructed to address exactly that gap. Business Acceleration Services are part of the support package described in the work programme, meaning successful applicants receive more than funding. The call's scope makes clear that the innovation must have the potential to create new markets or substantially change existing ones.

Preparation step. Prepare a rigorous, evidence-based account of your innovation's scientific or technological foundation and the specific market-creation potential it carries, ensuring the short proposal clearly articulates why conventional financing has been insufficient and how the combination of grant and investment funding would enable the scale-up trajectory described in the call scope.

Official topic page: HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01
