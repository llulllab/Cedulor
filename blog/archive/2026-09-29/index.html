EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 29 September 2026.

This is the edition of 29 September 2026. The dates and statuses below are as they stood on that day and may have changed since. Read the current edition.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation priorities, from materials science and border security to doctoral training, bioenergy, and social infrastructure. Each represents a distinct funding instrument with its own scope, expected outcomes, and preparation demands. Reviewing them carefully will help your team allocate effort well before any deadline approaches.

Advanced Materials for Miniaturised Energy Harvesting Systems

Open, closes 28 October 2026. HORIZON-EIC HORIZON EIC Grants. Indicative budget: EUR 0.5 to 4.0 million per grant, about 8 grants expected (2026).

This Pathfinder Challenge targets a genuinely ambitious frontier: identifying a new generation of advanced materials specifically for miniaturised energy harvesting modules, with a concrete target of reaching Technology Readiness Level 4 for the resulting energetically autonomous systems. The expected impact is explicitly tied to RePowerEU and the Green Deal, meaning proposals must demonstrate credible alignment with EU sustainability ambitions. Application areas cited include point-of-care diagnostics and smart sustainable cities, signalling that interdisciplinary teams bridging materials science and systems engineering will be best placed. The portfolio approach also means the evaluators will be looking for variety across selected projects, so articulating a distinctive materials pathway is strategically important.

Preparation step. Map your proposed advanced materials against the specific TRL 4 milestone described in the call, documenting the experimental evidence you already hold and the gaps that the project would close, so that the ambition of your concept is grounded in a credible progression rather than a general research vision.

Advanced border surveillance and situational awareness

Open, closes 5 November 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action addresses operational gaps in border surveillance, with a particular focus on detecting and, where appropriate, neutralising unmanned vehicles operating alone or in swarms during trafficking and smuggling activities. The scope explicitly extends to large-scale movements of people, including scenarios involving the instrumentalisation of irregular migration, which places this call at the intersection of security technology and fundamental rights obligations. The call requires that solutions be reliable and redundant without putting staff or society at risk, a demanding technical and ethical bar. Organisations with demonstrated capacity in sensor fusion, situational awareness systems, or border management operations are well positioned to lead or join a consortium here.

Preparation step. Draft a preliminary legal and ethical compliance framework that directly addresses the call's requirement to respect legal and ethical guidelines when detecting or neutralising unmanned vehicles, identifying which national and EU instruments govern your proposed technical approach and how your consortium will document compliance throughout the project.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-BM-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN-JD HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks - Joint Doctorates.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks in the Joint Doctorate format require deep structural cooperation between participating organisations, and the expected outcomes described here go well beyond training alone: they include boosted research and innovation capacity, increased internationalisation, and enhanced knowledge transfer between sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly values the conversion of doctoral research into products and services where relevant, making it attractive for consortia that include non-academic partners. Improved quality and sustainability of doctoral training programmes is listed as a direct outcome for participating organisations, which means institutions can use this call to reform supervision arrangements as well as to fund candidates. The internationalisation dimension signals that geographically diverse partnerships will carry weight in evaluation.

Preparation step. Identify the specific research theme and the cross-sectoral or cross-disciplinary knowledge transfer your Joint Doctorate will deliver, then map each participating organisation to a distinct contribution to that transfer so that the consortium structure visibly serves the training and research outcomes the call describes.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action calls for full demonstration of regional bioenergy value chains, from the sourcing of locally available sustainable solid biofuels derived from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes, through to high-efficiency combined heat and power conversion. The call explicitly references alignment with the Renewable Energy Directive, so proposals must show that their feedstock selection and conversion processes satisfy those provisions. A notable feature of the expected outcomes is the emphasis on creating opportunities for small and medium-sized enterprises and generating regional value, not simply demonstrating technical performance. Flexible renewable power and heat supply to local industries is also highlighted, broadening the potential industrial partners who could participate.

Preparation step. Conduct a regional feedstock assessment before the deadline to confirm that the biogenic residues and wastes you intend to use are genuinely locally to regionally sourced and qualify as sustainable under the Renewable Energy Directive, since the call makes this compliance a foundational condition of the demonstration rather than an ancillary consideration.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action responds directly to evidence that over 1.2 million people experience homelessness in Europe, with a substantial proportion in chronic situations lasting more than two years, and it calls for systemic interventions at neighbourhood level rather than individual-level responses alone. The scope makes clear that housing-led approaches, while important, are insufficient on their own, and that structural and multidimensional causes require coordinated social infrastructure and services. Proposals are expected to produce evidence that is made available to stakeholders involved in tackling homelessness, giving the work an explicit knowledge-dissemination function alongside its operational one. The neighbourhood scale of intervention is a defining feature of the call, meaning that local authorities and community organisations are natural consortium partners.

Preparation step. Select and characterise the specific neighbourhoods where your intervention will take place, documenting the existing social infrastructure gaps and the stakeholder landscape there, so that your proposal can demonstrate from the outset that the targeted sites reflect the structural homelessness challenges the call describes rather than contexts chosen for convenience.
