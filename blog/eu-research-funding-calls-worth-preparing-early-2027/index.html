EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 20 October 2026.

The calls below span a range of research and innovation instruments, from doctoral training networks to frontier research grants and place-based demonstration projects. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully, well before the deadline, will help your team shape a proposal that speaks directly to what evaluators are looking for.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are designed to raise the quality and international reach of doctoral training by bringing together organisations from different sectors and disciplines. The call explicitly targets improved employability for doctoral candidates both inside and outside academia, making it relevant to universities, research institutes, and industry partners alike. Enhanced cooperation and knowledge transfer between participating organisations is a stated outcome, which means the network design itself is as important as the individual research projects it supports.

Preparation step. Map the transferable skills and competences your proposed network will deliver, and identify which participating organisations from different sectors or disciplines will contribute to each, since the description places explicit weight on cross-sector knowledge transfer and the conversion of research ideas into products and services.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call focuses on demonstrating that regionally sourced solid biofuels from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes can feed high-efficiency combined heat and power plants in ways that comply with the Renewable Energy Directive. The expected outcomes link directly to European energy security, climate targets, and competitiveness, giving proposals a clear policy anchor. There is also an explicit opportunity for small and medium-sized enterprises to participate in regional value generation, which broadens the consortium profile beyond large research actors.

Preparation step. Identify a concrete regional value chain, from biogenic residue sourcing through to combined heat and power conversion, and document how each stage aligns with the sustainability provisions of the Renewable Energy Directive, since demonstrating that full chain compliance is a central requirement of the call.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

With over 1.2 million people estimated to experience homelessness in Europe, and nearly half of surveyed individuals in chronic homelessness for more than two years, this call addresses a documented and urgent social challenge at neighbourhood scale. The scope goes beyond housing-led approaches and calls for systemic interventions that target the structural and multidimensional causes of homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services. Generating evidence that is directly usable by stakeholders tackling homelessness is an explicit expected outcome, which means the proposal must build in a strong knowledge-transfer and stakeholder engagement dimension.

Preparation step. Define the specific neighbourhood-level social infrastructure and services your project will test, and establish from the outset how the evidence generated will be made accessible to the stakeholders responsible for tackling homelessness, since the call explicitly requires that this evidence reaches those actors.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports up to sixty months of frontier research, with the possibility of requesting additional funding for eligible costs that are necessary to carry out the proposed work, provided the request is duly justified. This makes it one of the most substantial individual research grants available to mid-career researchers in Europe.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear account of your track record of research independence, and draft a justification for any additional funding you may need beyond the standard maximum, since the description states explicitly that such requests must be duly justified in the proposal.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call is positioned as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, focused on scaling up novel solutions through territorial experimentation rather than initial piloting. It calls on countries, regions, and interregional partnerships to design governance and regulatory experimentation environments that can be replicated across a wide array of European Union funding instruments. The multi-layered governance dimension and the emphasis on turning analytical insights into best practices give this call a distinctive policy and territorial character.

Preparation step. Work with your regional or national partners to articulate the specific governance and regulatory experimentation mechanisms your project will demonstrate, and show how the insights produced can be replicated through other European Union funding instruments, since replicability across instruments is an explicit expected outcome of the call.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04
