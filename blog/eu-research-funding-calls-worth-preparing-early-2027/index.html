EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 29 October 2026.

The calls below span a wide range of research and innovation priorities, from deep geothermal resource assessment to urban homelessness, territorial mission scaling, cybersecurity for small businesses, and investigator-led frontier research. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help your team identify where your existing expertise and partnerships align most naturally.

Resource assessment for deep sedimentary and basement reservoirs

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 4.5 million per grant, about 4 grants expected (2026).

Deep sedimentary and basement reservoirs represent a largely untapped geothermal frontier, held back by low permeability, high drilling costs, and scarce subsurface data. This call directly targets the knowledge gaps that prevent developers from predicting reservoir structures and properties with confidence. By funding research into approaches such as Enhanced Geothermal Systems and closed-loop geothermal systems, it aims to increase drilling success rates and reduce the risk of induced seismicity. The focus on sustainable deployment, including effects on biodiversity, signals that multidisciplinary teams combining geoscience with environmental expertise will be well positioned.

Preparation step. Compile your consortium's existing subsurface datasets and modelling capabilities for deep sedimentary or basement formations, and identify which gaps in permeability prediction or seismic risk assessment your project would specifically close, since the call centres on building a knowledge base that developers can directly apply.

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

With over 1.2 million people estimated to experience homelessness in Europe and nearly half of surveyed individuals in chronic situations lasting more than two years, this call addresses a persistent structural problem rather than a short-term crisis. The scope explicitly calls for systemic interventions at neighbourhood level, focusing on social infrastructure and services rather than housing provision alone. Projects are expected to generate evidence that is directly usable by the stakeholders responsible for tackling homelessness, making real-world applicability a central criterion. Teams that can demonstrate genuine engagement with affected neighbourhoods and relevant service providers will be at an advantage.

Preparation step. Map the specific neighbourhood-level social infrastructure gaps your project will address, and establish early contact with local authorities or service organisations in the targeted areas, since the call expects improved availability and accessibility of services for homeless groups in concrete, identifiable locations.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The Consolidator Grant is designed for principal investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The grant supports ambitious, investigator-driven research over a substantial period, giving recipients the freedom to pursue a coherent scientific vision without being constrained by predefined application domains. The possibility of requesting additional funding beyond the standard maximum, when duly justified, allows for genuinely ambitious experimental or computational programmes. For researchers at this career stage, the grant represents a rare opportunity to define and lead a research agenda on their own terms.

Preparation step. Draft a concise narrative of the evidence for your research independence, drawing on publications, supervised researchers, and any independent funding you have held, since the description explicitly states that a Consolidator Grant principal investigator should have already shown such evidence. Reviewing this narrative with your research office well before the deadline will help you identify whether the case is sufficiently documented or needs to be strengthened.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call functions as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, oriented toward scaling up and wider deployment of solutions that have already shown promise. It invites territories including countries, regions, and interregional partnerships to design and demonstrate governance structures and regulatory experimentation that can support mission-relevant innovation. The emphasis on multi-layered governance and the conversion of analytical insights into replicable best practices means that projects must be grounded in real territorial contexts. The cross-mission framing also opens the door to consortia that can connect solutions across more than one Mission area.

Preparation step. Identify the specific territory or interregional partnership that will anchor your project and clarify which European Union Mission or Missions its governance experimentation will serve, since the call requires territories to actively design and demonstrate enabling environments rather than simply analyse them.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04

Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SME DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 5 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the practical gap between the development of advanced cybersecurity solutions and their actual adoption by small and medium-sized enterprises, which typically lack the internal capacity to evaluate and deploy such tools independently. It specifically calls for the deployment of market-ready, automated cybersecurity tools and services that help organisations prepare for, protect against, and respond to threats. The scope also covers innovative approaches to training employees to use these solutions, recognising that technology adoption depends as much on people as on products. Results from prior Horizon Europe projects are explicitly mentioned as a potential source of solutions to be taken to market.

Preparation step. Identify the specific automated cybersecurity tools or services your action will deploy, confirm they are market-ready rather than still at research stage, and outline how your dissemination approach will reach small and medium-sized enterprises, since the call centres on market uptake and practical deployment rather than further development.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME
