EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 18 October 2026.

The calls gathered here span doctoral training, energy demonstration, social innovation, frontier research, and territorial experimentation, reflecting the breadth of what European research and innovation funding can support. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully, well before the deadline, will help research offices and investigators position their proposals with precision.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are a primary instrument for building structured, cross-sectoral doctoral training at European scale. The call explicitly targets improvements in transferable skills, employability within and outside academia, and the conversion of research ideas into products and services. Participating organisations stand to gain through enhanced cooperation, knowledge transfer between disciplines and sectors, and boosted research and innovation capacity. The emphasis on internationalisation signals that consortia drawing partners from diverse national and sectoral backgrounds will be well positioned.

Preparation step. Map your prospective consortium against the call's dual focus on training quality and cross-sector knowledge transfer: for each partner, document what distinct disciplinary or sectoral perspective they contribute to doctoral supervision and what transferable-skills components they can credibly deliver.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call targets the full regional value chain for solid biofuel, from sourcing upgraded biogenic residues and wastes through to high-efficiency combined heat and power conversion, directly linking to the Renewable Energy Directive. The demonstration character of the action means that real-world performance evidence, not just modelling, is expected. The call also frames success in terms of regional value generation and competitiveness, which means economic and industrial partners are integral, not peripheral. Flexible renewable power and heat supply to local industries is an explicit outcome, broadening the relevance beyond utilities to process industries.

Preparation step. Before drafting, verify that your biofuel feedstocks and conversion pathway can be documented as compliant with the Renewable Energy Directive's sustainability provisions, since the call makes this compliance a prerequisite for the demonstration rather than an aspiration.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

The call responds to a documented European reality of over 1.2 million people experiencing homelessness, with nearly half in chronic situations lasting more than two years. It deliberately goes beyond housing-led approaches to require systemic interventions that address structural and multidimensional causes at the neighbourhood level. Evidence generation for stakeholders is a primary deliverable, meaning the project must produce findings that are useful to those actively working to tackle homelessness, not only to academic audiences. The neighbourhood scale is deliberate and constraining: proposals must be grounded in specific local social infrastructure and service contexts.

Preparation step. Identify the specific neighbourhoods where your intervention will operate and begin assembling evidence of existing social infrastructure gaps there, since the call requires that improved availability and accessibility of services for homeless groups in targeted neighbourhoods be a demonstrable project outcome.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are ready to consolidate their own team or programme. The description specifies a grant period of up to sixty months, with a primary funding ceiling and the possibility of requesting additional funding for eligible costs that are necessary to carry out the proposed work, provided the request is duly justified. This structure rewards researchers who can articulate both the scientific ambition of their programme and the resource logic behind it. The focus on consolidation means the proposal must reflect a coherent research identity, not simply a collection of projects.

Preparation step. Prepare a clear account of the evidence of your research independence to date, and draft a justification for any additional funding you intend to request, since the description explicitly states that such requests must be duly justified in the proposal.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call is explicitly framed as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, oriented toward scaling up and wider deployment of solutions that have already shown promise. The governance dimension is central: multi-layered governance and regulatory experimentation are listed as expected outcomes, not just implementation activities. Territories including countries, regions, and interregional partnerships are the primary actors called upon, which means proposals without genuine territorial anchoring will not meet the scope. The call also expects analytical insights to be converted into best practices replicable across a wide array of European Union funding instruments, giving results a reach beyond the project itself.

Preparation step. Engage your regional or national authority partners early to co-design the governance and regulatory experimentation components, since the call places territorial anchoring and multi-layered governance at the centre of what must be demonstrated rather than treating them as supporting context.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04
