EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 21 October 2026.

The calls below span a range of programmes and action types, from doctoral training networks to frontier research grants and place-based innovation. Reviewing each call's scope and expected outcomes carefully will help your team identify where your institution's strengths align best and where preparation should begin well before the deadline.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. HORIZON-TMA-MSCA-DN HORIZON TMA MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks are a direct instrument for building research and transferable skills in doctoral candidates while strengthening cooperation between sectors and disciplines across participating organisations. The call explicitly targets improved employability both inside and outside academia, making it relevant to consortia that span universities, industry, and other non-academic partners. Boosting internationalisation and increasing the integration of training and research activities are central outcomes, which means the network design itself carries significant weight in evaluation.

Preparation step. Map the transferable-skills and cross-sector training components your proposed network can genuinely offer, since the description foregrounds employability and knowledge transfer between sectors as core outcomes; document how each participating organisation contributes a distinct and complementary training environment.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

Resource assessment for deep sedimentary and basement reservoirs

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 4.5 million per grant, about 4 grants expected (2026).

Deep sedimentary and basement reservoirs represent a largely untapped geothermal resource precisely because they have been overlooked by conventional hydrocarbon exploration, leaving a significant knowledge gap that this call directly addresses. The description highlights the need to predict reservoir structures and properties in order to identify suitable locations for approaches such as Enhanced Geothermal Systems and closed-loop geothermal systems. Reducing the risk of induced seismicity and deploying geothermal energy in ways that protect biodiversity are explicit expected outcomes, signalling that environmental considerations must be integrated into the technical work from the outset.

Preparation step. Assemble the subsurface characterisation and drilling-data expertise your consortium brings to bear on low-permeability reservoir prediction, and prepare a clear account of how your methodology will address induced seismicity risk and environmental impacts, since both are named as required outcomes in the description.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-06

Understanding capital market dynamics for increased investment in New European Bauhaus projects in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This call focuses on a specific structural problem: the mismatch between the capital that New European Bauhaus neighbourhood projects can offer and the scale, return expectations, and risk appetite of available investment funds. Understanding that demand-supply discrepancy is positioned as a prerequisite for increasing investment in sustainable and inclusive neighbourhood transformation. The research is therefore oriented toward actionable insights for project promoters, public authorities, and investors rather than purely academic analysis.

Preparation step. Identify the capital market actors, public authorities, and project promoters your team can engage as research subjects or partners, and outline how your methodology will capture both the demand and supply sides of the investment gap described in the call, including the role of fund size and risk aversion.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-02

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for Principal Investigators who have already demonstrated research independence and are at the stage of consolidating their own team or programme. The description specifies a primary funding ceiling for a sixty-month period, with the possibility of requesting additional funding for eligible cost categories when duly justified in the proposal. The grant is awarded on the basis of scientific excellence, making the strength and originality of the proposed research programme the central consideration.

Preparation step. The Principal Investigator should compile a coherent record of research independence, including publications, group leadership, and any prior grants, and begin drafting the scientific narrative for the proposed programme well before the deadline, paying particular attention to how the work consolidates a distinct and original research direction.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This call serves as a strategic instrument for the second phase of the European Union Missions lifecycle, explicitly oriented toward scaling up novel solutions rather than piloting them for the first time. Territories including countries, regions, and interregional partnerships are invited to design and demonstrate governance and regulatory experimentation environments that can be replicated using a wide array of European Union funding instruments. The multi-layered governance requirement and the emphasis on territorial anchoring mean that strong public-public and public-private collaboration structures are central to a competitive proposal.

Preparation step. Work with your regional or national authority partners to document the governance structures already in place and identify the regulatory experimentation mechanisms that could be demonstrated and replicated, since the call specifically calls for territorial actors to design these enabling environments as a condition of participation.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04
