EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 29 October 2026.

This collection spans several distinct EU funding instruments, from large scale demonstration projects under Horizon Europe to European Research Council grants and Digital Europe programme actions. Researchers and research offices will find opportunities touching bioenergy, social infrastructure, frontier research, cybersecurity for small and medium enterprises, and biodiversity science. Planning early and aligning proposals tightly with each call's specific scope will be essential.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets the demonstration of fully sustainable regional bioenergy value chains, from locally sourced solid biofuels derived from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes through to high efficiency combined heat and power conversion. It directly supports the EU's climate and energy targets while opening economic opportunities for small and medium enterprises and strengthening European competitiveness in the bioenergy sector.

Preparation step. Map out a credible regional supply chain, identifying specific sources of biogenic residues and wastes available locally or regionally, and document how the proposed feedstock aligns with the provisions of the Renewable Energy Directive before drafting the demonstration plan.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

Homelessness affects over 1.2 million people in Europe, and nearly half of surveyed homeless individuals experience chronic homelessness lasting more than two years. This Innovation Action addresses the structural and multidimensional causes of homelessness by focusing on social infrastructure and services at the neighbourhood level, going beyond housing-led approaches alone.

Preparation step. Identify target neighbourhoods and conduct a baseline assessment of existing social infrastructure and services available to homeless groups, so the proposal can clearly articulate what systemic gaps the project will address and how accessibility will be improved.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for excellent Principal Investigators who are consolidating an independent research team or programme. It provides funding for up to 60 months, with the possibility of requesting additional funding when justified by the nature of the proposed work. This makes it a substantial instrument for researchers who have already demonstrated independence and wish to deepen or expand a distinctive line of inquiry.

Preparation step. Begin assembling a compelling track record of research independence, because the evaluation hinges on the Principal Investigator's demonstrated capacity to lead, and prepare a clear justification if additional funding beyond the standard grant ceiling is needed to carry out the proposed work.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SME DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 5 grants expected (2026).

This Digital Europe programme action focuses on strengthening the cybersecurity posture of European small and medium enterprises through the deployment of market ready, computationally powered cybersecurity solutions. It emphasizes not only tool deployment but also the integration of computational technologies into cybersecurity processes and employee training, creating a holistic approach to organizational resilience.

Preparation step. Survey existing computationally powered cybersecurity tools, including those developed under EU supported research and innovation projects, and assess which are mature enough for deployment in SME environments, since the call explicitly prioritizes market uptake and dissemination of ready solutions.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME

Better understanding, anticipating and addressing the biodiversity benefits and impacts of the clean and digital industries

Open, closes 14 January 2027. HORIZON-RIA HORIZON Research and Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.0 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

As Europe pursues reindustrialisation and decarbonisation, this Research and Innovation Action asks how clean and digital industries affect biodiversity across entire value chains, from design and operation to end of life and waste. It calls for understanding direct, indirect, and cumulative impacts across spatial and temporal dimensions, so that decision makers can choose transition options that do the least harm to ecosystems.

Preparation step. Assemble an interdisciplinary consortium that spans ecology, industrial systems analysis, and digital technology expertise, ensuring the team can credibly address the call's requirement to evaluate biodiversity impacts across multiple value chain stages and avoid burden shifting across ecosystems and regions.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NATURE-2027-01-02
