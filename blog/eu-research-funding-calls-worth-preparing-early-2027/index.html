EU Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Cedulor team. As of 30 October 2026.

This collection spans demonstration projects in bioenergy and social innovation, frontier research consolidation grants, place-based mission experimentation, and cybersecurity deployment for small and medium enterprises. Researchers and research offices working across Horizon Europe, the European Research Council, and Digital Europe will find distinct preparation angles for each opportunity.

Demonstration of solid biofuel supply and conversion to high efficiency CHP from fully sustainable regional value chains

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 6.8 million per grant, about 2 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action targets the demonstration of fully sustainable regional bioenergy value chains that convert locally sourced solid biofuels from upgraded biogenic residues and wastes into high efficiency combined heat and power. It directly supports EU climate and energy targets while creating opportunities for small and medium enterprises and strengthening European competitiveness in the bioenergy sector. The emphasis on regional sourcing and compliance with the Renewable Energy Directive makes it especially relevant for consortia that can mobilize local biomass supply networks.

Preparation step. Map out a concrete regional value chain, from the sourcing of biogenic residues and wastes through upgrading and conversion in a high efficiency combined heat and power plant, ensuring every link aligns with the provisions of the Renewable Energy Directive and demonstrating tangible benefits for local industries and small and medium enterprises.

Official topic page: HORIZON-CL5-2026-11-D3-05

Structurally addressing homelessness through coordinated social infrastructure and services in neighbourhoods

Open, closes 1 December 2026. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: around EUR 3.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

Homelessness affects over 1.2 million people in Europe, and nearly half of surveyed homeless individuals face chronic homelessness lasting more than two years. This Innovation Action goes beyond housing-led approaches by demanding systemic interventions in social infrastructure and services at the neighbourhood level. It seeks to produce evidence that stakeholders can use to structurally address the multidimensional causes of homelessness.

Preparation step. Identify specific neighbourhoods where coordinated social infrastructure and services can be demonstrated, and begin engaging local stakeholders (municipal authorities, social service providers, community organizations) who can contribute evidence on the structural causes of homelessness in those areas.

Official topic page: HORIZON-NEB-2026-01-BUSINESS-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. HORIZON-ERC HORIZON ERC Grants.

The European Research Council Consolidator Grant is designed for excellent Principal Investigators who are consolidating an independent research team or programme, requiring demonstrated evidence of research independence. It offers funding for up to 60 months, with the possibility of requesting additional funding when justified by the proposed work. This makes it a substantial instrument for researchers at a pivotal career stage seeking to deepen and expand a distinctive line of inquiry.

Preparation step. Prepare a compelling narrative that clearly documents your track record of research independence, and if your proposed work requires resources beyond the standard grant ceiling, draft a detailed justification for the additional funding request well before the deadline.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

Demonstrating place-based experimentation and innovation spaces as enabling environments for scaling EU Missions

Open, closes 13 January 2027. HORIZON-IA HORIZON Innovation Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 1.0 to 1.5 million per grant, about 3 grants expected (2026).

This Innovation Action serves as a strategic instrument for the transition of EU Missions into their scaling phase, focused on wider deployment of mission-relevant solutions across territories. It calls on countries, regions, and interregional partnerships to design governance frameworks, regulatory experimentation, and innovative public-public and public-private collaboration models. The emphasis on turning analytical insights into territorial experimentation and replicable best practices distinguishes it from purely research-oriented calls.

Preparation step. Begin assembling a coalition of territorial actors (regional governments, interregional partnerships, public agencies) that can credibly demonstrate multi-layered governance and regulatory experimentation, and outline how your proposed enabling environment will translate novel solutions into practices replicable across multiple EU funding instruments.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MISS-2026-07-CROSS-04

Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions

Open, closes 14 January 2027. DIGITAL-JU-SME DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions. Indicative budget: EUR 3.0 to 5.0 million per grant, about 5 grants expected (2026).

This Digital Europe action focuses on strengthening the cybersecurity posture of European small and medium enterprises through the deployment of market-ready, AI-powered cybersecurity solutions. It covers a broad scope: preparing, protecting, and responding to cybersecurity threats while also integrating trustworthy AI into cybersecurity processes. The call explicitly encourages uptake of results from EU-supported research and innovation projects, linking prior Horizon Europe outputs to real-world deployment.

Preparation step. Survey existing AI-powered cybersecurity tools, particularly those developed under EU-supported research and innovation projects, and design a deployment plan that shows how these solutions will be integrated into SME cybersecurity processes, including employee training on the use of AI for threat detection and response.

Official topic page: DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME

Deadlines, budgets, and call identifiers are drawn from the EU Funding and Tenders Portal and were verified against it on the date shown above. Confirm each call's current status on its own topic page before you rely on it. EU funding calls close at 17:00 Brussels time on their stated deadline.
